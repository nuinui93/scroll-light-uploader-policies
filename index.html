Private desktop publishing utility

Your own videos.
Your own channel.

Scroll and Light Uploader helps one creator publish locally produced videos to the creator’s authorized YouTube channels. It runs as a command-line program on the owner’s Mac. It is not a hosted service, a YouTube replacement, or a product sold to other users.

Uses YouTube API Services

The owner chooses a local video, title, description, visibility, thumbnail, captions, audience designation and synthetic-media disclosure. Before uploading, the client retrieves the authorized channel from YouTube and displays the selected settings for confirmation.

How it works

  1. Read the privacy policy and terms.
  2. Authorize the desktop client on Google’s own consent screen. The client does not request a Google password.
  3. Choose the exact upload files and settings in a local JSON file, including the expected channel ID.
  4. Review the channel and visibility. Confirm the upload explicitly.
  5. Use YouTube Studio for settings this client does not implement, including scheduled publication.

Owner controls

python3 youtube_api.py --help
python3 youtube_api.py channel
python3 youtube_api.py review UPLOAD.json
python3 youtube_api.py upload UPLOAD.json
python3 youtube_api.py disconnect

The connection can also be revoked from Google account permissions. Disconnecting does not delete uploaded YouTube videos or the owner’s original local media.
